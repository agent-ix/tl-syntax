# tl-syntax v0.1 source-release assurance profile

## Decision Boundary

This profile supports a human decision about one identified v0.1 source
revision. It does not accredit downstream parsers, evaluators, rewriters,
monitors, later revisions, or a crates.io publication.

PGM-01 classifies `tl-syntax` as linked runtime. Any consuming project must
verify the exact deployed crate, feature set, target, configuration, and
intended use; this profile confers no project validation or accreditation.

## Impact Scenarios

The principal adverse scenarios are silent acceptance of malformed formula
structure and loss or substitution of the semantic-profile identity at an
exchange boundary. Both can produce incorrect downstream temporal results.

## Evidence Policy

Evidence must identify the source revision, command or inspection, tool
versions, outcome, limitations, and requirement tags. Failed, skipped, pending,
and unsupported results remain distinct from passing evidence. Agent-produced
source and reviews require human code and release review.

Generated evidence is retained by Quoin, under the schemas Quoin packages and
the digests it computes at seal time. This repository defines no envelope of its
own. The `quire.derivation-evidence/v1` records its deleted collector wrote were
themselves deleted under `agent-ix/tl-syntax#12`; none was rewritten, and no
claim that they still verify survives them.

## Exceptions

No exception is implicit. A release exception must identify its owner, affected
requirement and impact scenario, rationale, expiry, and human acceptance record.
